# Zentel Network Service - Business Analysis Report

## Question 1: Overall Business Performance

### Summary

Zentel processed 5,998 tickets over 6 weeks. The overall business health is CRITICAL with a health score of 0%.

### Key Metrics

- **Response SLA Pass Rate:** 17.26% (Target: >90%)
- **Resolution SLA Pass Rate:** 58.25% (Target: >80%)
- **Average Response Time:** 265 seconds (Target: ≤10 seconds)
- **Average Resolution Time:** 144.9 minutes (Target: ≤180 minutes)
- **Escalation Rate:** 41.75% (Target: <20%)

### Findings

1. The business goal is to respond in 10 seconds or less. The actual average response time is 265 seconds → 4 minutes and 25 seconds.
Only 17% of tickets met the 10-second target. Performance is critically bad.

2. The target is to fix the issue within 3 hours. The actual average resolution is 144 minutes (~2.4 hours) which is OK.
BUT only 58% of tickets were resolved within target. Still marked critical because anything below 80% is usually considered poor in service industries.

3. Almost half the tickets were escalated (meaning the frontline team could not handle them). Healthy systems stay under 20%.
This suggests skill gaps, bad routing, or overload.

### Business Impact

This analysis shows poor business performance in Zentel as a business. The business is responding too slow,
fixing issues inconsistently, has too many escalations, bad quality and a bad trend that's getting worse.

The organization's support performance should be improved.
